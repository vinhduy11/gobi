Gobi App Icons

Five directions for the app icon, shown large and at 128, 64, 32 and 16 pixels on light and dark backgrounds. Say which one you want, or mix two, and I will build the real icon set.

1. Dunes

Dusk sky over two dune ridges. No letter, reads as a desert at any size.

2. G ring

A bold G drawn as one stroke on Go blue. Strongest at 16 px.

3. Prompt

A terminal prompt with a dune line underneath: Go as a command line tool.

4. Sun G

The G is the sun rising over the dunes. Name and language in one picture.

5. Layers

Three teal bands shaped like dunes. Quiet, fits next to Xcode and Terminal.

Go blue and desert sand come from the name: a Go tool called Gobi. Shapes are plain paths, so they export to every size macOS needs without relying on any font.
